# Russian 🇷🇺 placement notes

The item bank is `placement.json`: 30 multiple-choice items, 6 per level from A1 to C1, run adaptively.

## What the items cover
- **A1:** survival chunks (*меня зовут, я не знаю*), no present-tense "to be" (*где файл?*), prepositional after в, gender on adjectives, в vs на. Every prompt and choice has translit, so no Cyrillic is needed.
- **A2:** accusative objects (*ошибку*), -ся on tech verbs (*запускается*), possessive gender, в/на with tech nouns (*на сайте*), the *у меня есть* chunk, and aspect in requests. Choices still carry translit.
- **B1:** perfective result (*уже сделал*), ходить vs идти, *нет* + genitive, *мне нужно*, -ся, *на GitHub*. Cyrillic only.
- **B2:** instrumental after *с*, imperfective for repeated actions, genitive plural after numbers, prefixed motion verbs (*ушёл*), past-tense gender on soft-sign nouns, *актуальный*.
- **C1:** formal register, passive participles, *точный* vs *аккуратный*, case government (*пользоваться* + instrumental, *требовать* + genitive), passive -ся with an instrumental agent.

## Distractors
Every wrong choice is a documented English-speaker or heritage-speaker error: nominative where another case belongs, a missing -ся, an extra *есть*, the wrong aspect, a unidirectional motion verb where a habitual one belongs, English calques (*я имею вопрос*), and false friends.

## Best predictors of level
- **Case beyond nominative and accusative** (genitive, dative, instrumental). Noun case is the #1 L2 Russian grammar error (13.2% in RULEC-GEC), and dative/instrumental come last.
- **Aspect** separates B1 from C1. Lower levels overuse the imperfective; higher levels overuse the perfective (Apresjan 2024).
- **-ся** is the best A2 → B1 signal for tech users.
- Motion verbs and false friends are weaker predictors: they are drilled early but rarely used in tech chat.
