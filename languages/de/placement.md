# German 🇩🇪 placement notes

The adaptive bank is `placement.json`: 30 multiple-choice items, 6 per level from A1 to C1. The test runs 5 rounds of 3 items, and the CLI grades it.

## What each level covers
- **A1:** basic pronouns, der/die/das on common nouns, verb conjugation, and first chunks ("Ich brauche…", "Ich verstehe nicht").
- **A2:** V2 inversion, haben/sein in the Perfekt, separable verbs and the verb bracket, modal + infinitive, first false friends.
- **B1:** verb-final order after weil/dass, dative/accusative after prepositions, the dative verb *helfen*.
- **B2:** adjective endings, passive, relative pronouns, Konjunktiv II, two-way prepositions, genitive.
- **C1:** register and nominal style, extended participles, collocations, the double infinitive.

## Best predictors of level
- **Word order** (V2, then verb-final). It follows a fixed developmental sequence (Pienemann 1998), so a miss here places the learner reliably.
- **Case after prepositions** separates A2 from B1, because case is acquired late (Diehl et al. 2000).
- **Gender** is learned word by word and says little about level. Weight it lightly.
